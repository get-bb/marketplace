## A little art in your everyday life

Take a short break from work to discover a museum painting and notice something new.
Art Day brings one work into BB each day, with a story and a detail to look for.
Your ratings and favorites gradually guide future discoveries.
The sidebar thumbnail shows the current painting.

## A daily museum visit

Open a dedicated page with a large painting, its artist, year and museum,
a short Russian story, and a detail to look for. Enlarge or download the
reproduction and follow its credited source.

## Learn your taste

Rate works from 1 to 5, choose what you liked and save favorites. The next
fresh search considers those signals while leaving room for discoveries.
Today, Favorites and My Taste are separate tabs. Profiles stay independent
between BB installations. The interface and stories currently use Russian.

## Automatic selection

The schedule turns on when installed. A new museum day begins at 09:00
Europe/Paris, including daylight saving. BB must be running; after downtime
the plugin catches up. You can turn selection off in the page or settings.
At most three attempts run per day. Ordinary automatic retries are at least an hour
apart; a confirmed timeout can retry immediately; manual retries share the same daily limit. Failures keep the previous
painting's original date.

## Sources and requirements

Fresh research uses only Google Arts & Culture and Wikimedia Commons.
Reproductions come from Wikimedia and require verified Public Domain or CC0
rights. Only the current painting and favorites keep their full cards and
images; other shown works become compact history. No future catalogue is kept.

Requires BB 0.6.157+, internet and a configured BB agent with web research.
No separate login or API key is required. Agent work uses your provider's
subscription or usage limits and normal permission controls. BB and the agent
provider may retain their usual task transcripts. The plugin sends no messages
to other people and includes no personal profile or credentials.

A selection attempt is limited to 20 minutes. A timed-out worker must be confirmed stopped before an immediate retry; the three-attempt daily limit still applies. If stopping cannot be confirmed, no overlapping worker is started.
