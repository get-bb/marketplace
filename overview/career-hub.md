## Keep your search together

Track applications, maintain a complete career profile, and build relevant CVs for each role.

## What you get

- Paste a job from chat; track its history, stages and next steps.
- A detailed Master CV with manual editing and reusable experience.
- Multiple PDF, DOCX and text CV sources, reviewed before merging.
- Interview cases that feed back into your profile.
- Target-specific wording, ATS/AI checks, saved versions and PDF downloads.
- Drop a reference CV for section order and classic/compact spacing.
- An embedded assistant at the bottom of Profile, CV Builder and Interview prep.
- Separate personal and demo workspaces stored by BB.

## AI and requirements

The bottom assistant uses your BB provider and native chat controls. Your first Send creates a persistent conversation; opening the panel does not start one. Ask for profile, CV or preparation edits. Source facts remain reviewable; reference CVs supply layout preferences, not candidate facts. Applications are recorded manually.
Requires BB 0.48+. Scanned PDFs need OCR/text. LinkedIn supports a URL plus pasted text or an uploaded export; complete live profile fetching is unavailable.
CLI: `bb career status` and `bb career export`.
