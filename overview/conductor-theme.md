## What you get

Applies the Conductor workspace color palette across the entire bb interface, featuring deep roasted espresso surfaces, warm message cards, Geist typography, and high-contrast semantic accents.

## Design system

- Deep espresso canvas (`#151110`) with subtle warmth for reduced eye fatigue
- Warm espresso message bubbles and cards (`#2a1e1d`) with defined stone borders (`#3c3635`)
- Bone-white body typography (`#ece9e7`) rendered in Geist Sans and Geist Mono
- Emerald green interactive buttons, links, and active PR indicators (`#3eb15f` / `#49db7e`)
- Terracotta symbol highlights (`#d8a599`) and soft cyan branch accents (`#81ddf9`)
- Complete matching light mode variant with bone surfaces and dark coffee ink

## How it works

Once installed, the Conductor theme is available directly in the bb Theme selector. Switch themes at any time from the Command Palette or the Theme Preview panel.

## Requirements

- bb version 0.40.0 or later
- No external services or accounts required
