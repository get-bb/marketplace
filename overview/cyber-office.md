## A team, not a pile of threads

You talk to one agent: Stas, the coordinator. He splits your task, writes a brief for each specialist, and collects the results. Rams, the critic, checks the work before it comes back to you. He cannot be switched off.

## Who it is for

- You run several agent threads and lose track of who is doing what.
- You are a founder, a marketer, or a small agency, and you want ready roles, not a framework.
- You have two subscriptions and want one model to write and another to criticize.
- You want work accepted on evidence: a file, a check, a fact.

## What you see

- **The office.** Every role is a pixel cyborg at a desk, grouped by stage. You see who is working, who is waiting for you, and who hit an error.
- **Question cards.** When the team needs a decision you get one card with options and a default.
- **Result cards.** Every reply starts with status, the result in one line, what is needed from you, files, and what was checked.

## The team

Six roles are on by default: coordinator, researcher, engineer, designer, copywriter, critic. Six more are one switch away: analyst, strategist, SEO, video and voice, paid traffic, scout.

## Make it yours

- Rename the cyborgs, change their look and glow color.
- Pick a provider, a model, and an effort level for each role.
- Add your own agents at the empty desks.
- Hand each role your installed skills from a picker grouped by category, with recommendations for the role.

## First run

Ask in any thread: `Set up my Cyber Office team`. The `team-setup` skill reads your project, your skills, and your providers, then proposes a team and three first tasks. Nothing is applied until you say yes.

To see the whole chain without touching your work, add an empty folder as a project named `Demo: Breeze Coffee` and press **Test run**. It runs on the cheapest model with a spend cap and a stop button.

## Good to know

- Every role is a BB thread and uses your own provider quota.
- The plugin sends nothing outside your computer and needs no account or API key.
- English by default, Russian in Settings.
- Foreman, scout, and shift handoff are experimental and off by default.
- Tools are registered as `team_*`. If another plugin uses the same names, keep one of the two enabled.
