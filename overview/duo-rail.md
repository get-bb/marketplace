The iPhone Duo keeps a strip down one side of each display for the camera, the clock and the status icons: 84 points on the folded cover screen, about 85 unfolded. Those take its top, and the Dynamic Island grows down it during a Live Activity; bb leaves the rest blank. Duo Rail stands a column of symbol-only buttons on the bottom of the strip, just above the home indicator, stacking upward.

## What you get

- **Back, New thread and More** out of the box, on both displays. More lists your five most recent threads (a dot marks one waiting for you), Forward and Reload bb.
- **Your own buttons**: in the plugin's settings, choose each display's buttons, their order, their symbols (any of the 181 bb draws, picked from a small searchable popover beside the name) and what they do. A button can go back or forward, open a new thread in a project you pick, a thread, a project, your recent threads or a web address, run an action from bb's thread menu (Pin, Archive, another plugin's) on the thread in view, run a `bb` command picked from every plugin's commands and bb's own and show its result, reload bb, or open a menu of its own. Export and Import copy them between bbs.
- **One layout everywhere**: it is kept on your bb server, so every device follows it.
- **The right side or the left**: a mirrored device puts the strip, and the buttons, on the left.
- **Out of bb's way**: the buttons sit only in the strip bb leaves empty, take only their own taps, stay over a thread's side pane, and step aside when bb's sidebar drawer, a sheet or a menu opens over them.

## When it shows

Only on the Duo: a side inset on exactly one side and no top inset. The folded cover screen gets bb's phone layout and its own buttons; the unfolded display gets bb's wide layout and its own. A desktop, an ordinary iPhone, an iPad and Split View show nothing.

## Settings

Space for the camera, folded (default 175 points) and unfolded (default 110): how far down from the top the buttons may reach; the rail hides when they would reach higher. Raise one to keep the buttons clear of a taller island. The buttons themselves are edited in the Buttons section. Show debug numbers (off) puts small text above the top button with the screen's insets and sizes, updated live.

## Not included

Search, opening the sidebar, bb's own pages, other plugins' pages and bb's command palette: bb gives plugins no way to reach them from the rail yet.

## Requirements

bb 0.49 or later and an iPhone Duo with bb's iOS app. No account, service or API key. A button that runs a `bb` command needs bb's command-line tool on the bb server's machine (bb's Settings → General → Command line).
