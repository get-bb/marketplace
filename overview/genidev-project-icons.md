## What you get

Give a project an icon in the BB sidebar. Open the project's menu and choose **Icon**. Pick one from the built-in collection or import a PNG, JPEG or WebP image.

The icon is shown next to the project's name. Built-in icons can animate while an agent is working and show a waiting state when the project needs your input. You can turn animation off in the plugin's settings.

Choose the empty option to clear an icon. Your custom images and selections are stored on your BB server.
