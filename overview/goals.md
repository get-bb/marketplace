## Keep your priorities in view

Maintain one ordered list of personal goals across your BB projects. Add a goal, edit its wording, and drag it up or down to change its priority. Completed goals remain in a separate section and are excluded from the active list agents read.

## Ask for a decision check

Ask an agent to compare an idea, answer or plan against your goals. It reads the current list through `bb goals show` and explains the fit, tradeoffs and possible improvements. Goals do not start agent work automatically.

## Requirements and data

The list works locally in BB and starts empty. Up to 30 goals can be stored, with 800 characters per goal. Updates are saved automatically with revision checks. An agent uses its configured provider when you ask it to evaluate your goals; no extra service account is required for the list.
