Speak to a BB thread on your iPhone and hear the reply aloud.

## How it works

Open Hands-Free, choose a thread, and tap the microphone to dictate. Tap again to send. When the agent finishes, Hands-Free reads its reply while the page stays open. You can open Thread activity to see what's happening.

## Before you use it

Requires BB 0.43 or newer, microphone access, and a configured voice-transcription service. It works while BB is open and your phone is unlocked. Dictation is **sent immediately without a chance to review it**, so don't use it for secrets or risky instructions. If the microphone is unavailable, you can use keyboard dictation. Spoken replies use your device voice, or the optional separately installed [Read Aloud plugin](https://github.com/csells/bb-plugins/tree/HEAD/plugins/read-aloud). Audio or speech may go through your configured transcription, browser, or voice provider.
