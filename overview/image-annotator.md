Draw on images before sending them to your agent.

Click a draft image attachment to open the editor. Use a pen, highlighter,
arrow, or rectangle to point out the parts you mean. Red is selected by
default; other colors, stroke sizes, undo/redo, and zoom are available.

Apply replaces the draft attachment with an annotated PNG, so the agent sees
the marks as part of the image. Cancel keeps the original. Hold Shift while
clicking to use BB's regular image preview.

Works on unsent image attachments with mouse, touch, or pen. No external
service or account is required. Original stored files are kept unchanged.
