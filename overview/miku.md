Miku walks around BB, plays idle animations, and reacts when work finishes or needs attention. Drag her to a new spot, turn on Stay in place, or hide her from the command palette. Muting her voice keeps the text bubbles visible.

For personalized comments, choose a brain project and configure a hidden thread with your preferred provider and model. You can set periodic check-ins or leave her responding only to app events. Without an enabled brain, she uses scripted reactions.

Agent-powered comments use your configured BB provider and its account or usage allowance. Optional musical speech is synthesized locally and needs FFmpeg with Flite and Rubber Band on the BB server. Browser audio requires a click in BB before playback.
