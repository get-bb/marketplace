## Navigation on the left, threads on the right

Nav Rail moves BB's navigation into a vertical rail of icon buttons on the left edge of the sidebar, stacked under the sidebar toggle on a slightly darker strip. **New thread** moves into the header row, left-aligned with the thread rows under it. Rail buttons match BB's own header controls: 28px with 16px icons on desktop, 36px with 20px icons on phones. Hover labels and selected states identify each destination.

## Keep your navigation preferences

Your existing order and hidden items carry over, because Nav Rail reads them from BB. Open **More (…) → Customize sidebar**, or right-click an icon, to reorder destinations or hide them. Hidden destinations stay reachable from More. Right-click also offers **Open in split** and **View details** for plugin panels, and panel counts show as small badges.

## Enable or restore

Installing Nav Rail selects it for both **Navigation** and **Header** under **Settings → Appearance**. Set **Header** back to **bb (built-in)** to keep New thread as a row above the thread list instead. Set **Navigation** to **Navigation**, or disable the plugin, to restore BB's rows.

## Requirements

Requires Plugin SDK 0.5.15 or later. No extra service, account, or API key is needed.
