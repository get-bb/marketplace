## Learn something in bb

Say what you want to learn: “I want to read a Go board well enough to play my first game.” Common Concept is a tutor that turns that goal into a map of skills and teaches them one lesson at a time in bb. The tutor calls your goal and the route toward it a **Mission**.

Each lesson gets its own thread. If a question takes you down a side path, you can keep it in a child thread and return to the lesson. Open **Learn** to pick up where you left off, see the skills and lessons ahead, or review something you've already studied.

## Get started

Install Common Concept, then run `bb concept init ~/learn` on a machine connected to bb. Open **Learn**, choose **+ New Mission**, and tell the tutor what you want to learn.

Tutoring runs through pi on the model you configure. Your model provider’s usage charges apply. The map, your progress, and your transcripts are saved in `~/learn`, a directory you own. Local tutoring doesn’t require a Common Concept account.

More about Common Concept at [mmon.co/ncept](https://mmon.co/ncept).
