## What you get

An **Open in new window** item in every thread's actions menu: the "..." menu
on a sidebar row, its right-click menu, and the thread header's menu. Pick it
and that thread opens the way a `target="_blank"` link
does: a new browser tab or window, following your browser's settings.

## How it works

The action opens the thread's own bb URL, the one **Copy link** copies, with
an ordinary
`target="_blank"` link click. The new page has no link back to the one that opened it.

## Requirements

Nothing to configure. There are no settings, no commands, and no network
access beyond loading bb itself in the new window.
