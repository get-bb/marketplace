Pinch to make text bigger or smaller, anywhere in bb.

## What you get

- **Text size**: pinch anywhere in the app to scale text from 75% to 200%.
- **Terminal text size**: terminals follow the text size, and a pinch over a
  terminal fine-tunes it from 8px to 32px. The terminal re-wraps to the new
  size once you let go.
- **Choose what scales**: by default only the chat, composer and terminals,
  while the sidebar and headers stay at their normal size; or switch to the
  whole app.
- **Relative sizes**: settings make the composer and terminal larger or smaller
  than the chat text, from half to double.
- Works with a touchscreen (iPhone, iPad, Android), a trackpad, or Ctrl + mouse wheel.
- A Settings section with A−/A+ and Reset buttons for each size.

## How it works

The pinched sizes are saved on each device, so your phone and your desktop keep
their own. The relative sizes and what scales are plugin settings shared by every
device. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
