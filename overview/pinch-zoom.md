Pinch to make text bigger or smaller, anywhere in bb.

## What you get

- **App text size**: pinch anywhere in the app to scale its text from 75% to 200%.
- **Terminal text size**: pinch over a terminal to change its font from 8px to
  32px. The terminal re-wraps to the new size once you let go.
- Works with a touchscreen (iPhone, iPad, Android), a trackpad, or Ctrl + mouse wheel.
- A Settings section with A−/A+ and Reset buttons for each size.

## How it works

Sizes are saved on each device, so your phone and your desktop keep their own
sizes. Nothing is sent to the server.
