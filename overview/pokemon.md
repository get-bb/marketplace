## Catch a Pokémon for every thread you archive

Each time you archive a thread, a Poké Ball wobbles in the corner of BB and a random Pokémon pops out. Any of the 1,025 species can appear, and duplicates count.

## Fill out your Pokédex

The **Pokédex** page shows how many species you've caught, your recent catches, and a grid of every Pokémon, with silhouettes for the ones you haven't found. Select a catch to read its entry: artwork, types, flavor text, stats, abilities, its cry, and the threads you caught it from.

## How it works

Species data comes from [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co) and is cached on your BB server. Only top-level threads earn a catch, once per thread, so re-archiving a thread doesn't earn another.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.43 or later and Plugin SDK 0.4.88 or later.
