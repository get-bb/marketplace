## Organize your sidebar

Keep your most-used groups in view and organize related work in a collapsible folder. In Custom, the folder holds sections. In Projects or Machines, it holds projects or machines. Each mode remembers its own name, icon, and contents.

## Move groups and find threads

Drag a heading onto the folder, or open the folder menu and choose which groups belong inside. The same menu lets you rename the folder and choose an icon. Drag a group above the folder to bring it back into the main list.

Switch to Active to see ongoing work, Input to find threads waiting for you, or Unread to catch up on updates. The filters include threads in collapsed groups and use the same icons, spacing, and thread menu as the All tab.

## Keep long sections short

Turn on **Enable Load more** in Sidebar Folders settings to limit the starting number of threads in custom sections and Threads. Use **Load more** to reveal the next batch or **Show less** to return to the limit. Pinned has its own optional limit. The open thread stays visible, and child threads stay with their parent.

## Recognize threads by their icons

Open a thread's menu and choose **Change icon**. The grid has 71 icons for topics such as meetings, digests, design, workouts, and expenses. Pick one of nine colors or keep the default gray. The picker uses 12 columns on desktop and switches to eight with larger targets on phones. BB saves each thread's icon and color across windows. Choose **Provider default** and save to restore the agent's icon.

## Requirements

Use BB 0.50.1 or later with Plugin SDK 0.6.157 or later and the built-in thread list. The plugin stores its settings in BB and requires no external service.
