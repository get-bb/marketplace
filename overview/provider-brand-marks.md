## What you get

Recognise Claude Code, Codex, Pi, Hermes Agent and OpenCode by provider-specific
colours in bb. Colours adapt to your existing palette in light and dark mode;
Cursor keeps its monochrome appearance. Your theme stays in place.

## Customisation

CSS custom properties let your theme override a provider's colour, lightness
or chroma. Companion rules align colours in bb-usage-page and usage-tracker
when those plugins are installed; neither companion is required.

## Requirements and compatibility

Requires bb 0.41 or newer. No external account or service is required.
OpenCode's colour is an approximation; corrections are welcome. The stylesheet
uses internal bb DOM attributes. A future bb update can rename those attributes
and make the affected colour rules stop applying.
