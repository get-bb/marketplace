Let your agents answer questions from Qlik Sense Enterprise reports — with
the numbers the Qlik engine calculates, and a link to the sheet so people can
check them.

## What you get

- A **Qlik Sense** page in the sidebar: sign in with your Qlik login, tick the
  apps agents may read, and mark the sheets to start from with a note on what
  each one answers.
- Agent tools that list sheets and objects, look up field values, and read any
  table, chart, pivot or KPI as a table with the filters the agent passes.
- *Can't find a report?* — paste a link, see whether your account can open it,
  and copy a ready access request for the app owner.
- The page is in English and Russian, with an EN | RU switch at the top.

## Company data stays with approved models

You choose which provider and model pairs may read Qlik data; nothing is
allowed at first. Other agents never see the Qlik tools, a thread that switches
to another model loses them, and a thread that already holds Qlik answers
cannot be continued by a model outside your list.

## Your password

It stays on the machine where agents run — in the macOS Keychain, encrypted
with your Windows account, or on Linux in a file only your user can read —
never on the bb server. If Qlik rejects it, the
plugin stops signing in until you enter it again, so the domain does not lock
your account.
