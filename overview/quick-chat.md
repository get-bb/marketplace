## A quick chat window, anywhere in BB

Quick Chat opens a small chat window in the corner of BB for questions that don't need a project. Press **Mod+Shift+K**, run **Quick chat: open** from the command palette, or click the chat icon in the sidebar footer.

## Stays out of your sidebar

Quick chats run in a personal workspace and don't show up in your thread list. The window lists your recent chats so you can pick one back up. If a chat turns into real work, click **Open as thread** to move it into the sidebar.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.42 or later and Plugin SDK 0.4.56 or later.
