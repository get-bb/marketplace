## Start a thread from anywhere

Quick Compose opens BB's new-thread composer in a floating window over the page you're on. Press **Mod+Shift+L** (rebindable in Settings → Keyboard) or run **Quick compose: new thread** from the command palette.

## Stay where you are

The composer starts on the project you're viewing. When you submit, it closes and the thread starts in the background, with a toast to open it. Press Escape or click outside to dismiss it without losing your draft, or the project and environment you picked.

## Hand off a thread

On a thread, press **Mod+Shift+H** or run **Quick compose: hand off this thread**. The composer opens with an `@thread` mention of the thread you're on and reuses its environment by default.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.42 or later and Plugin SDK 0.4.92 or later.
