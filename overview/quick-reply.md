Copying quotes into the message box makes reviewing a plan feel like work. Replying “looks good” saves typing, but leaves the agent guessing which details you actually approved.

## Click through the whole reply

Hover a paragraph, list item, table row or code block. Agree, disagree or ask for more detail, then move to the next line. Nothing to type unless you want to add a comment.

Give every point a decision while it is in front of you. Precise feedback means less guesswork and fewer wrong turns.

## What you get

- **Four line buttons:** agree, disagree, tell me more and comment.
- **One click per line:** green, red or amber edges show what you have covered. Click another verdict to change it, or the same one to remove it.
- **Optional comments:** explain a verdict or leave a comment on its own.
- **One message back:** **Submit replies** sends all your marks together, or queues them while the agent is working. The bar counts your marks; **Clear** drops them.

## What the agent receives

An ordinary message from you: each marked line quoted, followed by “Agreed.”, “Disagreed.” or “Please explain this in more detail.” and any comment. Skipped lines are left out.

Works with whichever agent your thread uses. Adds no skill, command or tool.

## Requirements

**BB 0.46 or later.** No account or external service. Marks stay in your BB window across thread switches and reloads until sent or cleared.
