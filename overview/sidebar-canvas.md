## Your daily background

- Fresh subjects, with palette and style guided by your 1–10 ratings.
- Light and dark tinting keeps project and chat names readable.
- A compact menu to request another wallpaper, keep your favorite, or hide the background.
- Your own schedule and time zone; a missed daily run catches up when BB starts. Confirmed access failures can retry one and two hours later.
- Images and preferences stay in BB storage. Generation is handled by the agent and tools you connect and may incur their normal charges.

Choose an agent with an image-generation tool in plugin settings. Automatic generation is off until you enable it. If generation fails, your previous wallpaper stays in place.

Experimental: standard BB project-list sidebar supported; custom sidebar replacements may not be supported.
