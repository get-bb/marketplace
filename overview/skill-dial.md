## What you get

- **One tap instead of typing.** Pick the skills you use most, such as code review, debugging or planning, and each gets a chip with its emoji and label next to the send button. Chips appear in every thread and on the New thread page.
- **Insert or send.** An *Insert* chip puts the skill, plus any arguments you preset, at the start of your draft so you can add details before sending. A *Send* chip submits it immediately, together with whatever you already typed.
- **Selected text becomes the argument.** Select a passage in the chat, then tap a chip: the skill arrives with that text as its parameter. Select words inside your draft and the skill is placed right before them.
- **Room for as many chips as you want.** When they do not fit, the strip scrolls with the mouse wheel, trackpad or a swipe. The » toggle folds all chips into one, and the choice is remembered across reloads and devices. On narrow screens such as phones, chips shrink to their emoji.

## Set it up

Open Settings → Plugins → Skill Dial. For each chip choose a skill (suggestions come from the same list as bb's `/` menu, or type any name), an emoji, a label, Insert or Send, and optional arguments. Reorder or delete chips in the same list. The list applies to all projects.

## Good to know

Requires bb 0.49 or later. A chip is disabled, with a tooltip, when its skill is not available for the thread's provider. Chips are not shown in the editor for queued messages. No account, network service or API key: your chips are stored by the plugin on your bb server.
