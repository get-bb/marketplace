## Keep your place

Arrange pinned and inbox threads with drag and drop or `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down`. Your order persists, so status changes do not shuffle the list.

## Follow parent and child threads

Expand children beneath their parent and see descendant activity reflected on the parent card. Thread-header controls take you between related threads. Opening or searching for a child expands its parent automatically.

## Triage without losing work

- Snooze a thread for 30 minutes, 2 hours, 1 day, or 1 week.
- Settle quiet threads into a collapsed shelf without archiving them.
- Press `Ctrl+Alt+S` to settle the open thread, including while typing in the chat composer. On Mac, use `Control+Option+S`.
- On touch screens, swipe a card left to settle or right to choose a snooze time.
- Select several threads for bulk snooze, settle, or archive actions.

Active work and pending attention, including work in descendants, prevent parking. An optional Inactive shelf collects quiet, unpinned threads after a delay you choose; new activity brings them back.

## Read the context on each card

See the project, branch, working-tree state, pull-request number and state, and provider mark alongside the thread status. Git indicators distinguish clean, untracked, uncommitted, and unmerged work. Pull-request indicators distinguish draft, open, merged, and closed states.

Filter by project, search with BB's sidebar search, and rename threads inline.

## Requirements

Requires BB 0.37.0 or later. Select **Inbox Sidebar** in **Settings → Appearance → Sidebar** after installation. No separate service or account is required.
