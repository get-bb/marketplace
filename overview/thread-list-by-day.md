## Pick up yesterday’s conversation

See conversations across projects in one timeline, newest activity first. A running thread appears in Today even if you started it days ago. Today and Yesterday show every conversation in those groups. Pinned threads stay at the top, and your existing sections and thread menus are available from the same sidebar.

## Keep the week in view

Recent days start with six threads each. Older conversations expand twenty at a time. Collapse any group to focus on one day. BB remembers that choice in the current window. Long titles fit on one line. Date headers scroll with the list, and the selected thread stays visible in an expanded day even beyond its usual row limit. Drag-and-drop and right-click actions keep working.

## Set up your sidebar

Choose **Claude-Like Threads** in **Settings → Appearance**. In chronological organization, sort by Updated to group by activity, or Created to group by start date. The alphabetical view uses a plain title list.
