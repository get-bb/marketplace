## Pick up yesterday’s conversation

Switch between projects all day and still find the thread you were working on. Today and Yesterday show every conversation in those groups. Pinned threads stay at the top, and your existing sections and thread menus are available from the same sidebar.

## Keep the week in view

Recent days start with six threads each. Older conversations expand twenty at a time. Collapse any group to focus on one day. BB remembers that choice in the current window.

## Set up your sidebar

Choose **Claude-Like Threads** in **Settings → Appearance**. In chronological organization, sort by Updated to group by activity, or Created to group by start date. The alphabetical view uses a plain title list.
