## What you get

Chat widget puts Git, subagents, created materials and attachments next to your conversation. Click an icon to expand its section, and click it again to collapse it. The open section stays highlighted. Hover over an icon to see its label.

- **Attachments and created materials.** See files from your messages and results created in the thread. Hover over a file icon to see its name.
- **Git.** See the current branch, local changes and the subjects of recent commits. Git needs a repository in the thread's working environment.
- **Subagents.** See active and completed agents for this thread.
- **View all.** Open the matching section of BB's side panel. The results panel also links to its archive.

The widget appears in the thread context card. The plugin enables BB's thread context card experiment on installation.
