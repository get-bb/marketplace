Find your place in a long thread. Threadline puts a slim rail on the right edge
of every conversation, one marker for each prompt you sent.

## What you get

- A prompt rail on the right edge, one marker per prompt. The prompt you are
  reading stays highlighted as you scroll.
- A hover popover that lists every prompt's preview, so you can scan the whole
  conversation and find the one you want.
- One-click jumps. Click a marker or a preview to jump to the start of that
  prompt, even when it is far off-screen.
- A position setting that places the rail at the top, middle, or bottom, just
  above the chat input.

## How it works

The rail reads the thread's own conversation outline, so the previews match what
you sent. Jumps drive BB's real timeline, including off-screen messages, so
nothing is duplicated and nothing leaves your machine.

## Requirements

Runs inside BB. No account, network, or external service. The rail shows up only
in a thread that has at least one prompt.
