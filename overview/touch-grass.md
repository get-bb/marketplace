## The meadow

Overgrown starts with short grass. Give it 40 minutes and the blades reach about a third of the chat. Open the sliders above the message box to see the countdown, choose 20, 40 or 60 minutes, or press **Grow now** to bring up the whole meadow. Each chat remembers its cuts while BB is closed.

## Play

Wait about 15 seconds in a new meadow for the first wildflower. Tap the glowing firefly or brush across it once to grow another. Daisies and cornflowers carry narrow leaves. Clover stays low, with leaves in threes. Bees land on the flower heads. Low strawberry plants sometimes grow between them, with berries hanging on their own stalks.

On a phone, hold a finger against the blades, slide sideways and let go. Grass and flower stems bend under your finger and spring back when you let go. Swipe up or down over the meadow to scroll the chat. Pick up the scythe on the left to cut at finger height, or send the mower across the whole strip. Sweep through a flower to cut it individually. The mower clears grass, flowers and strawberry plants together. New plants can return after about two minutes.

Choose **Compact** or **Tall** for a smaller strip that grows with the conversation. Each chat keeps its own flowers, berries and cut paths on this device. The size, regrowth period and visitor settings apply across chats. The plugin uses BB’s local event log and local browser storage.

## По-русски

Пришло время погладить траву. Цветы растут сами. Коснись светлячка, чтобы распустился ещё один, или проведи пальцем по стеблям. Косой можно срезать один цветок, газонокосилка расчистит весь луг. [Скриншоты и правила игры](https://popkov.dev/apps/touch-grass/)
