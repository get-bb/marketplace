## The meadow

Overgrown starts with short grass. Give it 40 minutes and the blades reach about a third of the chat. Open the sliders above the message box to see the countdown, choose 20, 40 or 60 minutes, or press **Grow now** to bring up the whole meadow. Each chat remembers its cuts while BB is closed.

## Play

Brush a glowing firefly back and forth to wake it. A flower opens where it was sleeping. Bees visit the flowers, and a bee landing on a strawberry blossom leaves a ripe berry.

On a phone, hold a finger against the blades, slide sideways and let go. The grass parts and springs back. Swipe up or down over the meadow to scroll the chat. Pick up the scythe on the left to cut at finger height, or send the mower across the whole strip. A cut opens a path to the messages behind it.

Choose **Compact** or **Tall** for a smaller strip that grows with the conversation. Each chat keeps its own flowers, berries and cut paths on this device. The size, regrowth period and visitor settings apply across chats. The plugin uses BB’s local event log and local browser storage.
