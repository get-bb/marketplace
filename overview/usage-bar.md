## Headroom at a glance

Keep provider quota chips just above the sidebar footer. Each chip shows the reported weekly window, or the first available window, with its reset time. Click the strip for all available windows and recorded token totals.

## Independent by design

Uses BB provider authentication and reporting directly. No Usage Limits plugin is required. When both are installed, Usage Bar owns the strip and Usage Limits supplies its own footer shortcut.

## What the counters mean

Only reporting providers and hosts appear. Token totals reflect history recorded in BB, not a billing statement. Deleted history cannot be recovered. Snapshots refresh periodically and after thread activity.
