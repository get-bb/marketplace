## Make the handoffs visible

Build agent workflows on a canvas: connect steps, choose specialist profiles and inspect each worker's conversation. Branches run when their inputs are ready. Final-output steps bring the result together.

## Independent by design

Includes local step profiles and sample workflows. No other Kapustin plugin is required. Optionally import Agent Roles profiles and legacy workflows as independent copies. Refresh imports explicitly; removing the companion leaves your copies usable.

## Before running

Configure a BB provider and review step instructions and permissions. Runs consume that provider's quota. A failed or timed-out step fails the run; you can inspect the completed steps and their outputs.
